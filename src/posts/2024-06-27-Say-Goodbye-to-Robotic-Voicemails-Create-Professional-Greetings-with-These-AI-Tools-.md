**FAQs: Your Burning Voicemail Questions, Answered**

**1. What should I say in a professional voicemail greeting?**

Keep it concise and professional! Start with a warm greeting, state your name and company (if applicable), and let callers know you're unavailable. Finish by inviting them to leave a message and assuring them you'll get back to them ASAP.

**2. Should I use my own voice or an AI voice?**

It depends! Using your own voice adds a personal touch, but AI voices offer consistency and a polished sound. Experiment and see what works best for you.

**3. Can I add background music to my voicemail greeting?**

Absolutely! Many voicemail greeting generators (like our winner, Perfect Greet!) offer background music options. Just choose something subtle and professional that won't distract from your message.

**4. How long should my voicemail greeting be?**

Aim for 15-25 seconds max. People are busy, so get to the point and make it easy for them to leave a message.

**5. Can I change my voicemail greeting regularly?**

You bet! Keep things fresh by updating your greeting for holidays, special announcements, or just to switch things up.
