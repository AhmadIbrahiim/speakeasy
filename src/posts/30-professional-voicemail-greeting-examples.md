## <a name="what-to-include-in-a-business-voicemail-greeting"></a>What to Include in a Business Voicemail Greeting

A professional voicemail greeting should include the following elements:

- **A Greeting:** Start with a warm and friendly greeting.
- **Your Name:** Clearly state your name.
- **Your Company’s Name:** Mention your company to establish context.
- **Reason for Missing the Call:** Briefly explain why you can't take the call.
- **Callback Information:** Provide a timeframe for when the caller can expect a return call.
- **Alternative Contact Information:** Offer another way to reach you or a team member if the matter is urgent.

## <a name="30-professional-voicemail-greeting-examples"></a> 30 Professional Voicemail Greeting Examples

### <a name="standard-voicemail-greeting-examples"></a>Standard Voicemail Greeting Examples

1. <a name="standard-voicemail-greeting-example"></a>**Standard Voicemail Greeting Example**

   - "Hi! Thanks for calling [Your Name] at [Company Name]. I'm currently unavailable. Please leave a message, and I'll get back to you as soon as possible. Have a great day!"

2. <a name="callback-voicemail-greeting"></a>**Callback Voicemail Greeting Example**

   - "You've reached [Company Name]. We can't take your call right now. Please leave your name, number, and the best time to call you back. We'll get in touch soon."

3. <a name="text-message-voicemail-greeting"></a>**Text Message Voicemail Greeting Example**

   - "Hey, it's [Your Name] at [Company Name]. I can't wait to chat. Leave your name and number, or better yet, send me a text with the best time to reach you. Looking forward to hearing from you."

4. <a name="email-voicemail-greeting"></a>**Email Voicemail Greeting Example**
   - "Hi, you've reached [Your Name] at [Company Name]. For a quick response, please email me at [Email Address]. If it's not urgent, leave a message with your name and number. Have a great day."

### <a name="specific-business-voicemail-greeting-samples"></a>Specific Business Voicemail Greeting Samples

5. <a name="customer-support-voicemail-greeting"></a>**Customer Support Voicemail Greeting Example**

   - "Hello, you've reached the Customer Support department at [Company Name]. We're unable to take your call. Please leave your name, phone number, and reason for your call. We'll call you back as soon as possible."

6. <a name="sales-team-voicemail-greeting"></a>**Sales Team Voicemail Greeting Example**

   - "You've reached the sales team at [Company Name]. We're currently assisting other customers. Please leave your name, contact information, and a brief description of your needs. We'll call you back soon."

7. <a name="product-launch-voicemail-greeting"></a>**Product Launch Voicemail Greeting Example**

- "Thanks for calling [Company Name]. We're excited about our new [Product/Offer]. Leave your name, number, and the best time to call, and we'll follow up with you."

8. <a name="business-hours-voicemail-greeting"></a>**Business Hours Voicemail Greeting Example**

   - "Hi, you've reached [Company Name]. We're open from [Hours] [Time Zone] Monday through Friday. Please leave your name, number, and reason for your call. We'll get back to you soon."

9. <a name="vacation-voicemail-greeting"></a>**Vacation Voicemail Greeting Example**

- "Hi, you've reached [Your Name] at [Company Name]. I'm on vacation from [Start Date] to [End Date]. For immediate assistance, contact [Alternative Contact] at [Phone Number]. Thank you."

10. **Conference Voicemail Greeting Example**
    - "Hello, this is [Your Name]. I'm attending the [Conference Name] from [Start Date] to [End Date]. Please leave your name, message, and contact information. I'll get back to you as soon as possible."

15. **Real Estate Firm Voicemail Greeting Example**
    - "You've reached [Firm Name]. We're assisting other clients. Please leave your message, and we'll return your call as soon as possible."

### <a name="holiday-voicemail-greeting-examples"></a>Holiday Voicemail Greeting Examples

16. **Company Shutdown Voicemail Greeting**

    - "Hello, and thank you for calling [Company Name]. We're closed for our annual holiday shutdown from [Start Date] to [End Date]. For urgent matters, email [Emergency Contact Email]. Happy holidays!"

17. **Long Weekend Voicemail Greeting**

    - "Thank you for calling [Company Name]. We're closed for the long weekend from [Start Date] to [End Date]. We'll resume regular hours on [Next Business Day]. For urgent matters, email [Contact Email]."

18. **Team Offsite Voicemail Greeting**

    - "Hello! You've reached [Company Name]. Our team is attending an offsite meeting from [Start Date] to [End Date]. Please leave a message or email [Contact Email]. We'll respond as soon as we're back."

19. **One-Day Holiday Voicemail Greeting**

    - "Hi, and thank you for calling [Company Name]. We're closed for [Holiday Name] today, [Date]. We'll return to normal hours on [Next Business Day]. For urgent matters, email [Contact Email]."

20. **Detailed Holiday Voicemail Greeting**
    - "Happy holidays. Thanks for calling [Company Name]. Our hours are different during the holiday season. Please listen carefully to the following changes. For urgent matters, email [Support Email]. Enjoy your holiday season!"

### <a name="emergency-voicemail-greeting-examples"></a>Emergency Voicemail Greeting Examples

21. **Personal Emergency Voicemail Greeting**

    - "Hello, you've reached [Your Name] at [Your Company]. I'm out of the office due to an emergency and will return on [Expected Return Date]. For urgent matters, contact [Alternative Contact] at [Phone Number]."

22. **Natural Disaster Voicemail Greeting**

    - "Hello, you've reached [Your Name] at [Your Company]. Due to [Disaster Name], our operations are suspended. For urgent matters, contact [Emergency Contact] at [Their Contact Information]."

23. **Technical Emergency Voicemail Greeting**

    - "Thank you for calling. This is [Your Name] from [Your Company]. We're experiencing a technical issue. For immediate assistance, contact [Alternate Contact] at [Phone Number]."

24. **High Call Volume Voicemail Greeting**

    - "Hello, thank you for calling [Your Company]. We're experiencing high call volumes. Please leave your name and number. We'll get back to you as soon as possible. For urgent matters, email [Emergency Email]."

3. **Provide Context in Emergencies:** Inform customers about urgent situations.
4. **Appear More Professional:** A detailed voicemail message with a clear call to action builds customer confidence.
