## The Perfect Structure (With Examples!) 📝

Let me show you exactly how to structure your greeting:

### 1. Start with Primary Language (20 seconds)

```
"Hello! You've reached [Your Company].
We value your call and will respond within 24 hours.
Please wait for your preferred language..."
```

### 2. Add Second Language (20 seconds)

```
"Bonjour! Vous avez contacté [Your Company].
Nous apprécions votre appel et vous répondrons dans les 24 heures.
Veuillez laisser un message après le bip."
```

### 3. Other Languages (if needed)

- Spanish: "¡Hola! Ha contactado con..."
- German: "Hallo! Sie haben..."
- Chinese: "您好！您已联系到..."
- (Add what you need)

## Pro Tips for Each Language 🎯

### 🇺🇸 English Tips

- Use clear American or British accent
- Speak at medium speed
- Keep it simple and clear

### 🇫🇷 French Tips

- Use standard French pronunciation
- Maintain professional tone
- Include polite expressions

### 🌏 Other Languages

- Get native speaker help
- Record each part separately
- Test with real people

## Common Mistakes (And How to Fix Them!) ⚠️

### ❌ Mistake 1: Too Much Information

**Wrong:** "Hello, you've reached ABC Company. Our office hours are 9 AM to 5 PM, Monday through Friday. We're located at..."

**Right:** "Hello, you've reached ABC Company. Please leave a message, and we'll call back within 24 hours."

### ❌ Mistake 2: Bad Sound Quality

**Solution:**

- Use Speakeasy's professional recording tools
- Add soft background music
- Record in a quiet room

### ❌ Mistake 3: Wrong Language Order

**Fix It:**

1. Most common local language first
2. International business language second
3. Other languages by usage
