# Perfect Greet vs Google Voice - Which One Should You Choose?

Hi friends! 👋 Today, we're going to look at two popular ways to make voicemail greetings: Perfect Greet and Google Voice. I'll make it super easy to understand which one is better for you. Let's check them out!

## Quick Overview 🔍

First, let's see what each service offers:

### Perfect Greet

- 100% free
- No sign-up needed
- Professional background music
- Easy to use
- Focus on voicemail greetings

### Google Voice

- Needs Google account
- Some features cost money
- Part of bigger Google system
- More complicated to use
- Many different features

## Price Comparison 💰

Let's talk about money first:

### Perfect Greet Costs:

- Everything is free
- No credit card needed
- No hidden fees
- No premium features to pay for

### Google Voice Costs:

- Basic personal use: Free
- Business features: $10-30 per month
- Needs Google Workspace subscription
- International calls cost extra

## Ease of Use 🎯

How easy is it to make a greeting?

### Perfect Greet:

1. Go to website
2. Record your message
3. Add background music
4. Download and use!

### Google Voice:

1. Create Google account
2. Set up Google Voice
3. Find voicemail settings
4. Record without music
5. Set up forwarding
